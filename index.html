Germany's Riester successor, in force since 29 May 2026, live from 1 January 2027

From 2027 the state tops up every euro you put into ETFs.

Pay in up to 1,800 € a year, get up to 540 € back in cash subsidies plus 300 € per child, a tax deduction on top, and no tax on gains while you save. The catch is a lock-up until 65, a full income-tax hit on the way out, and a cost cap that only covers one product variant.

Three years from idea to law

Suggested by an expert group in 2023, drafted twice (the first draft died with the Ampel coalition), rewritten in committee, passed in spring 2026, live in 2027. The Frühstart-Rente for children is a separate act still in parliament.

done today, 2 Oct 2026 still ahead
  • 2023
    Suggested. The Fokusgruppe private Altersvorsorge (BMF, BMAS, BMWK, academics, consumer groups, industry) recommends a guarantee-free Altersvorsorgedepot.
  • 30 Sep 2024
    First draft. Lindner's pAV-Reformgesetz (even single stocks allowed, 20 ¢/€ Zulage). Dies when the coalition breaks on 6 Nov 2024.
  • Apr 2025
    Mandate. CDU/CSU–SPD coalition agreement: replace Riester, add a Frühstart-Rente for children.
  • 1 Dec 2025
    Ministry draft. Referentenentwurf: 30 ¢/€ Zulage, 1.5 % cost cap. Cabinet adopts it on 17 Dec 2025.
  • 26 Feb – 16 Mar 2026
    Parliament. First reading, then a committee hearing where the 1.5 % cap is called "embarrassing by international standards".
  • 25–27 Mar 2026
    Rewritten and passed. Committee switches to 50 %/25 % Zulage, cuts the cap to 1.0 %, adds the self-employed and a state-run Standarddepot. Bundestag passes with CDU/CSU and SPD.
  • 8 May 2026
    Legalised. Bundesrat consents; published 29 May 2026 as BGBl. I 2026 Nr. 156. The law is in force.
  • Aug – Sep 2026
    Market forms. Scalable, Smartbroker+, finanzen.net Zero announce 0 % depot fees; Deka, quirion publish fund costs. Frühstart-Rente draft gets its first reading on 25 Sep 2026.
  • today
    2 Oct 2026. BZSt certification under way; final fee schedules of ING, DKB, comdirect, Trade Republic still pending. Pre-registration open at several brokers.
  • 1 Jan 2027
    In effect. Contracts can be signed, new subsidy rules apply, no more new Riester contracts. Frühstart-Rente planned to start (if passed in time); public Standarddepot likely later.

Three contract types, one rule list

All are "certified Altersvorsorgeverträge" (BZSt). The subsidies and taxes are identical; what differs is guarantee, fund choice and whether the cost cap applies.

Altersvorsorgedepot-Vertrag

No guarantee. You (if the contract offers the choice) or the provider pick funds from the legal list. Up to 100 % equities. Costs are not capped — this is where "premium" fee structures will appear.

highest expected returnno cost capbest for 15+ years to go

Standarddepot-Vertrag Altersvorsorge

Mandatory offer for every provider. Two preset funds (one risky, one defensive), automatic shift into the defensive fund in the years before payout unless you opt out. Effective cost capped at 1.0 % p.a. Sold online without advice. A state-run version is authorised but needs a regulation that does not exist yet.

cost cap 1.0 %no decisions needed0 % fee at several brokers

Garantieprodukt

The provider guarantees 80 % or 100 % of your contributions at payout start. To fund that, a large share sits in bonds, so expected return is lower. Typically insurer-run, with acquisition and administration costs. Reasonable only with few years to go.

80 % or 100 % guaranteelower return, higher cost

Allowed (Positivliste, exhaustive)

  • UCITS funds and ETFs, PRIIPs risk class ≤ 5 of 7 (world ETFs are class 4)
  • Open public AIFs and ELTIFs, risk class ≤ 5
  • Euro bonds of Bund, Länder, municipalities, public-law bodies, Euro states, EU, Euratom, EIB, EFSF
  • A settlement account inside the contract

Not allowed

  • Single stocks (the 2024 draft had them, the law does not)
  • Crypto and crypto ETPs
  • Leveraged products, certificates, CFDs, warrants
  • Funds in risk class 6–7, real estate, commodities, closed funds
  • Transferring ETF units you already own — only cash goes in

Fund units are segregated assets (Sondervermögen) and survive a provider insolvency without a cap; only cash on the settlement account relies on the 100,000 € deposit guarantee.

Not tied to your employer

The AVD is a private contract in your own name. Changing jobs changes nothing. What is checked, year by year, is whether you belong to the eligible group in that contribution year.

Pick your situation

Directly eligible

Full Grundzulage (up to 540 €), Kinderzulage, youth bonus and the Sonderausgabenabzug. Your employer is not involved at any point; a job change needs no action.

  • Job change: contract, fund positions and all past subsidies stay untouched.
  • Years without eligibility: contributions are "unsubsidised" (later taxed only on the earnings share) or you let the contract rest. Every certified contract must allow pausing. No claw-back.
  • Protection: the subsidised capital is shielded from Grundsicherung means-testing and from seizure; unsubsidised excess is not.
  • Moving outside the EU/EEA during the payout phase counts as harmful use and triggers the claw-back.

Costs decide the outcome, not the subsidy

Ages, and how the money comes out

Starting

No minimum age in the law for adults — you need to be eligible (a 16-year-old Azubi qualifies). 200 € bonus if under 25. Children only via the Frühstart-Rente (from 6) or an unsubsidised Standarddepot. No upper age, but not while drawing a full old-age pension.

Payout window

Earliest 65 (or earlier if a statutory pension already starts earlier), latest 70. The contract must leave you a free-choice window of at least 5 years; tell the provider 3 months ahead. You may switch provider at this point, e.g. to an insurer for an annuity.

Forms

Up to 30 % as a lump sum, no strings. The rest as a payout plan running at least to 85 (remainder inheritable, nothing after the end) or a lifelong annuity (ends at death unless a 10/20-year guarantee period is agreed). Tiny balances (annuity ≤1.5 % of the SGB IV Bezugsgröße, roughly ≤20 k€ capital) can be paid out in full.

If you die: your wife, her depot, your son

The capital is inheritable in principle, but the subsidies are only kept if the money moves into the surviving spouse's own Altersvorsorgevertrag. Anyone else inherits after a claw-back.

Who inherits?
In which phase?
Transfer to her contract, no deductions

    Her own depot

    Untouched. Her Zulage, her contract terms and her payout window stay as they are. Your capital is added by transfer, which is not a contribution, so it does not consume her 1,800 € subsidised or 6,840 € hard limit. Income tax falls due only when she draws it; inheritance tax has a 500,000 € spouse allowance.

    Trap: if one spouse keeps an old Riester and the other opens a new AVD as an indirectly eligible partner, the new contract earns no Zulage. Both must be in the same system.

    Your son's own provision

    When tax is paid

    Deferred taxation: a deduction now, no tax while saving, income tax on the way out.

    Things that bite later

    The Zulage is not automatic

    Sign the Dauerzulagenantrag when you open the contract; the provider then applies every year at the ZfA using your Steuer-ID. Without it, the deadline is the end of the second year after the contribution year (2027 money → 31 Dec 2029). Report marriage, births, Kindergeld changes and self-employment promptly, or the ZfA reclaims later. Check the yearly statement.

    Existing Riester: never cancel, migrate

    Old contracts are grandfathered. You can keep the old rules, switch only the subsidy system by declaration, convert with the same provider, or transfer the capital to a new AVD without claw-back and without the old provider's consent. No way back. Cancelling triggers the claw-back; pausing does not. Riester stays better only for savers with several children who cannot afford 25 € a month. No new Riester contracts from 2027.

    Rürup, bAV and VL do not connect

    No transfer from a Basisrente. Company-pension contributions can still be subsidised the old Riester way; the AVD is not a new bAV channel. Vermögenswirksame Leistungen cannot be routed into an AVD.

    Home ownership is optional per contract

    Withdrawal for a self-used home (purchase, debt repayment, energy refurbishment, age-appropriate conversion) stays subsidy-neutral, but providers no longer have to offer it. Pick one that does if you might want it. Wohnförderkonto taxation is compressed to 5 years after payout start.

    Certification says nothing about quality

    From 2027 the BZSt certifies "under reservation of revocation" without a full pre-check so products can launch on day one. It confirms the contract follows the rules — not that it is cheap or good.

    What is still open

    Regulations detailing the Positivliste boundaries; the state-run Standarddepot (which entity, which funds — reportedly delayed); the Frühstart-Rente act (first reading 25 Sep 2026). Over 40 years tax rules will change; that risk exists for every retirement product.

    What the watchdogs say

    Verbraucherzentrale, Stiftung Warentest and Prof. Malmendier (at the hearing): good structure, but the 1 % cap is high by international standards and covers only the Standarddepot; expect a sales wave of expensive "premium" products. Advice: no hurry, avoid commission-based sales, compare effective costs, consider waiting for the public product.

    A plan for a well-paid employee with spouse and child

    1. Pre-register with one or two zero-fee brokers now; decide in Q1 2027 once certified terms are published, including Trade Republic, ING and DKB.
    2. From 2027, each spouse pays 1,800 € a year into their own contract (if your wife is not eligible herself, 120–360 € in her contract still earns up to 175 €), plus 300 € in whichever contract claims the Kinderzulage.
    3. Hold one broad world ETF (risk class 4, TER ≤ 0.2 %) in a free-choice AVD, or take the Standarddepot where its two funds are Vanguard-class cheap; opt out of the glide path if you want to stay invested.
    4. Keep everything above 1,800 € a year in a normal Depot: flexibility, cheaper payout taxation, clean inheritance to your son.
    5. For your son: Frühstart-Rente if born 2020 or later; otherwise a normal Junior-Depot (free access at 18) beats an unsubsidised, locked Standarddepot.
    6. At payout, prefer a plan over an annuity unless longevity insurance is the explicit goal; if annuity, insist on a 20-year guarantee period for your wife.
